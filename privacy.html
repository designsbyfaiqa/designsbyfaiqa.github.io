Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

faeka uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, the artist faeka, with her own TikTok account.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission through TikTok Login, the tool accesses: basic profile information, the ability to upload or post videos to the owner's account, and the list of the owner's videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

How the data is used and stored

This information is used only to upload the owner's videos and measure how they perform. It is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the tool's data folder removes all stored information.
